Privacy Policy

Montessori Cozy Story and the Cartoon Studio Uploader tool. Last updated: 13 October 2026.

Who we are

Montessori Cozy Story is a YouTube channel run by Triardor Studio. Contact: triardor.studio@gmail.com.

What the Cartoon Studio Uploader does

Cartoon Studio Uploader is an internal tool that uploads videos created by Triardor Studio to our own YouTube channel and manages their titles, descriptions and settings. It uses the YouTube API Services. It is used only by Triardor Studio with our own Google account. It is not available to the public and it does not access anyone else's account.

Data we access

  • Basic information about our own YouTube channel (channel ID and name), to make sure videos go to the right channel.
  • Our own videos and their metadata, which we upload and manage.

We do not collect, store or share personal information about viewers, including children. Our channel is set as "made for kids" and we follow YouTube's rules for children's content.

How data is stored

The access token that lets the tool upload to our channel is stored only on our own computer. We do not sell, share or transfer any data obtained through the YouTube API Services to third parties.

Google and YouTube

By using YouTube content you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's use of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access granted to the tool can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. Questions: triardor.studio@gmail.com.
